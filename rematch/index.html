        Feature Map — what this page does today

        Current capabilities. Not a roadmap. The series stay on this device.

        • Names You and Rival, editable. The series on the glass uses the names as you type them. A shelved series keeps the names it had.
        • Series A game title, best of 3, 5, 7, or an odd custom from 1 to 21, and an optional stakes line. First to the majority clinches. 24 series on this phone.
        • Scoreboard The running score, large enough to settle an argument. You on gold, rival on ice.
        • Log a game You won or They won. An optional one-line note sticks to that game. Undo last game takes a mis-tap back, including a clincher.
        • Status Series open, a lead, tied mid-series, you clinched, or they clinched. The line under the score says how many wins are left to clinch.
        • History Past series stay on this phone. Open one to see the card. Shelve and start new moves an open series onto this shelf unchanged, score and all. An unfinished series can go back on the glass.
        • Remove Remove one series, or clear every series. Undo puts it back until the toast leaves. Names stay.
        • Snapshot share Packs the series on the glass, or one opened from history, into #r= or ?r=. Opening it offers Keep or Not now. A copy that still uses the default names You and Rival is labeled Sender and Them, so the sender's win does not read as yours. Keep leaves the names on this phone alone. It is not live sync. Nothing is uploaded.
        • On this device Store rematch-v1. Offline cache rematch-v2. No accounts, no analytics.

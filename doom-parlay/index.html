      Feature Map — what this page does today

      Current capabilities. Not a roadmap. The slip stays on this device.

      • Legs Add 2 to 8 legs. Remove any leg. Start over clears the slip and leaves history.
      • American or percent Each leg takes American odds (−110, +150) or a win percent. The toggle sets the default. A leading +/− is read as American. A % sign is read as percent.
      • Implied probability Each armed leg shows its implied win chance and the American equivalent.
      • Compound Parlay win chance is the product of the leg probabilities. That assumes the legs are independent. Real games are not. The American price of the whole parlay sits under the hero.
      • Doom meter The glass drains as the win chance collapses. Roast copy escalates with the meter. Entertainment only.
      • Labels Optional team, player, or market. Stored on this device. A snapshot you copy can carry them. Nothing is uploaded.
      • History Last 12 parlays on this device. Restore one back onto the glass.
      • Snapshot share Packs this slip into #p= or ?p=. A friend can open that copy. It is not live sync.
      • On this device Store doom-parlay-v1. Offline cache doom-parlay-v1. No accounts, no analytics, no sportsbook.

    Feature Map — what this page does today

    Current capabilities. Not a roadmap. The bombs stay on this device.

    • The sit Who you benched is required. Who you started instead is optional. Points are a number or a rough range, and they are required. A week or league note is optional. Whitespace is trimmed. Word order inside a name does not matter, and the typed text stays on the card. Markup and emoji stay as text. The note does not move the energy.
    • Bomb A theatrical BOMB card. Energy from DUD through NUCLEAR, one roast line, a stamp, a fallout meter, the benched name, the points, and the starter when you named one. The same sit gets the same card. The stamp and the meter follow the points. The meter is entertainment theater, not a projection and not a model.
    • Same sit The same benched name, starter, and points reading, once each name is split, trimmed, lowercased, and sorted, share one card. A range is the same forwards and backwards. About 32 and 32 share a card. Bombing them again brings that card back, including a re-bombed roast. The week note does not move the energy.
    • Re-bomb A new roast on that same energy. The stamp stays with the energy. The fallout stays with the points. The star stays. Undo re-bomb restores the previous line.
    • Star Mark a bomb you want on the starred filter. Undo star takes the mark off.
    • Undo The Undo button takes back the last bomb, re-bomb, or star on the open card. Remove and Clear all use the toast Undo until it leaves. So does a bomb that had to make room. A later note does not cover a toast that still has Undo.
    • History Up to 24 bombs on this phone. Filters are all and starred. Open one, remove one, or clear every bomb. Opening a row does not replace what you are typing. Undo puts them back until the toast leaves. A new bomb at the cap drops the oldest unstarred bomb. Starred bombs stay. If every bomb is starred, the board refuses another.
    • Snapshot share Copy link copies the open bomb into #n= in one tap. Copy shelf link packs every bomb. ?n= works if the token is in the query string. The link does not carry the star. Opening it offers Keep or Not now. Keep recomputes the energy, stamp, meter, and roast from the sit. A shelf link keeps what fits. The offer says what it adds, and how many oldest unstarred bombs it drops, before you tap. If nothing fits, the offer stays up. A duplicate says Already on this phone. The address bar is cleared on purpose. A bad token toasts. A same-tab hash change shows the offer. It is not live sync. Nothing is uploaded.
    • Load sample Justin Jefferson for 38.4, started a committee back, so there is a card to look at without typing.
    • On this device Store bench-bomb-v1. Offline cache bench-bomb-v1. The page is fetched from the network when you are online. Scripts and styles refresh in the background. A ?v= query is cached by pathname only. If storage is blocked, a banner says so. If a save cannot be read, the old value is backed up and a notice shows. No accounts, no analytics, no scoring API.

    Not in this app: fantasy advice, betting, money, projections, news, live scores, accounts, live sync, ads, or anything that phones home. Entertainment only. For the group chat.

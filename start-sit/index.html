    Feature Map — what this page does today

    Current capabilities. Not a roadmap. The verdicts stay on this device.

    • The fight Player A and Player B. A name is required, and whitespace is trimmed. Team / pos and a short gut note are optional. The two names have to differ.
    • Decide A theatrical card. One side is START, gold and green. The other is SIT, cooled gray and ice. One spicy line, plus a confidence meter. The meter is entertainment theater, not a projection and not a model.
    • Same pair The same names, tags, and notes always get the same call, in either slot order. A gut note with hot or cold words can nudge who starts. It is still a seeded costume.
    • Swap sides The two columns trade places. The same player still starts. Undo swap puts them back.
    • Rematch / Reroll A new seeded flavor on that same card. If the same player still starts, the line does not say the booth changed its mind. The star stays. Undo rematch restores the previous call.
    • Star Mark a verdict you want to find on the shelf. Undo star takes the mark off.
    • Undo The Undo button takes back the last decide, swap, rematch, or star on the open card. Remove and Clear all use the toast Undo until it leaves.
    • History Up to 24 verdicts on this phone. Deciding a pair that is already shelved, in either order, brings that card to the top instead of adding another. Open one to put it back on the card. Remove one, or clear every verdict. Undo puts them back until the toast leaves.
    • Snapshot share Copy link packs the open verdict, including one opened from the shelf, into #s=. ?s= works if the token is in the query string. Opening it offers Keep or Not now. Keep does not copy the sender's star. The address bar is cleared on purpose. A bad token toasts. A same-tab hash change shows the offer. It is not live sync. Nothing is uploaded.
    • Load sample Avery Knox over Nico Vale, so there is a card to look at without typing.
    • On this device Store start-sit-v1. Offline cache start-sit-v2. The page is fetched from the network when you are online. If storage is blocked, a banner says so. No accounts, no analytics.

    Not in this app: fantasy advice, betting, projections, news, live scores, accounts, live sync, ads, or anything that phones home. Entertainment only. For fun with friends.

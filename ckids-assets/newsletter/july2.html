A Blessing for the Week

“Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!”

— Psalm 133:1

As July continues, let us remain grateful and keep our hearts united as we follow Jesus’ direction and serve faithfully in our ministry.

We want to thank you for investing your time this summer to love and serve our children and families. Together, let us help create meaningful and joyful memories for the children this season, allowing them to experience the love of God through us.

As this week begins, may we be ready to love as God loves us and help make this summer truly remarkable for every child. Here are some important details for this week ahead.

      Feature Map

      What this rotation does today

      Current capabilities — not a roadmap. The roster stays on this device.

      • One group Team or class name plus an optional season label. Edit anytime.
      • Families Who can bring snacks. Optional dietary note (nut-free, dairy-free).
      • Snack dates Assign a date to a family. Change or remove it. Past dates stay for the rotation.
      • Today / next-up On-deck card and stamp so you see who is bringing this week in ten seconds.
      • Who has not gone Suggests the family that has not brought in longest. One tap assigns them.
      • Undo last assign Reverses the last date add, family change, or removal on this device.
      • Coach clipboard Clean print-friendly list — date, family, dietary flags.
      • Snapshot share Copies a compressed URL hash. Not live sync. Copy a fresh link after you edit.
      • Stays on this device localStorage key snack-duty-v1. Clear device wipes it. Offline via cache snack-duty-v1.

      Not in this app: accounts, a server, live team sync, a potluck dish list, or a packing bag.

          Who's due
          Least recent buyer sits in the hero. Same timestamp ties break alphabetically.

      • 
          Bought
          One tap on a person (or the due hero) stamps the time and advances the round.
        
        • 
        
      • 
          People
          Add, rename, or remove. Need at least two to track. Optional group label (Coffee / Drinks / Lunch).

      • 
          Undo
          Undo the last Bought. Timestamps roll back from on-device history.

          Encodes the current group into the URL (#r= / ?r=). A friend can tap Use this group. Not live sync.

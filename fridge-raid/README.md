# Fridge Raid

90-second lunchbox arcade: raid the fridge, pack fresh food, toss anything spoiled or not food. Play at [bryanralston.github.io/fridge-raid](https://bryanralston.github.io/fridge-raid/). No signup.

## How to play

Tap or drag items into a kid lunchbox, or into **TOSS**. Fresh lunch food scores. Treats are okay for fewer points. Spoiled leftovers and junk (keys, phone, remote) wreck your combo if they land in a lunchbox.

## Score

- Fresh food: +100, builds combo (caps at x6)
- Treat: +35
- Full lunchbox (4 slots): +300 and a new empty box
- Toss spoiled or junk: +75
- Pack spoiled: −200, shake, combo dies
- Pack junk: −250, shake, combo dies
- Toss fresh food: −50

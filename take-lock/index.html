    Feature Map — what this page does today

    Current capabilities. Not a roadmap. The vault stays on this device.

    • Seal A matchup, the take, an optional side tag, and an unlock time. Presets are 15 minutes, 1 hour, tonight at 7, tomorrow at 1, ready now, or an exact minute. 24 takes on this device.
    • Sealed The take text is not drawn. The card shows a seal, a countdown, and the unlock time.
    • Watching If this tab is open when the clock hits, the seal cracks and the take is exposed.
    • Reveal If the clock hit while the tab was closed, the card waits. Break the seal to expose it. Reveal does nothing early.
    • Exposed The take in full, on a card built to be screenshotted.
    • Edit Change the words, the side, or the clock. Move the unlock into the future and the take seals again.
    • Remove Remove one take, or clear the vault. Undo puts it back until the toast leaves.
    • Filters All, sealed (still shut, or waiting on Reveal), and exposed. A vault link still packs every take.
    • Snapshot share Packs the vault or one card into #t= or ?t=. A friend can keep that copy or merge it. It is not live sync. Nothing is uploaded.
    • On this device Store take-lock-v1. Offline cache take-lock-v1. No accounts, no odds feed, no analytics.

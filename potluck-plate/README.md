# Potluck Plate

Local-first dish signup for church potlucks, school events, and team dinners. Claim a category, list what you’re bringing, share a snapshot link.

## Share links

**Copy share link** packs the current event into the URL hash. Opening that link loads the snapshot on another device. It is **not** live sync — later edits on one phone do not push to another unless you share a fresh link.

## Privacy

Everything stays in this browser’s `localStorage`. No accounts, no server, no secrets in the repo. Demo names are fictional.

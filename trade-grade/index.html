    Feature Map — what this page does today

    Current capabilities. Not a roadmap. The grades stay on this device.

    • The trade What you give and what you get. A name or a package is required, and whitespace is trimmed. A league or team note is optional. The two sides have to differ. Markup and emoji stay as text.
    • Grade A theatrical letter card. A+ through F, one roast line, a confidence meter, and the give ↔ get summary. The meter is entertainment theater, not a projection and not a model.
    • Same pair The same give and get, once normalized, always get the same letter and the same roast. The league note does not move the grade. Grading that pair again brings the card back to the top.
    • Re-grade A new seeded roast on that same letter. The star stays. Undo re-grade restores the previous line.
    • Star Mark a grade you want on the starred filter. Undo star takes the mark off.
    • Undo The Undo button takes back the last grade, re-grade, or star on the open card. Remove and Clear all use the toast Undo until it leaves. So does a grade that had to make room.
    • History Up to 24 grades on this phone. Filters are all and starred. Open one, remove one, or clear every grade. Undo puts them back until the toast leaves. A new grade at the cap drops the oldest unstarred grade. Starred grades stay.
    • Snapshot share Copy link packs the open grade, including one opened from the shelf, into #g=. Copy shelf link packs every grade. ?g= works if the token is in the query string. Opening it offers Keep or Not now. Keep does not copy the sender's star. A duplicate says Already on this phone. The address bar is cleared on purpose. A bad token toasts. A same-tab hash change shows the offer. It is not live sync. Nothing is uploaded.
    • Load sample Ja'Marr Chase for a flex dart and vibes, so there is a card to look at without typing.
    • On this device Store trade-grade-v1. Offline cache trade-grade-v1. The page is fetched from the network when you are online. If storage is blocked, a banner says so. No accounts, no analytics.

    Not in this app: fantasy advice, betting, projections, news, live scores, accounts, live sync, ads, or anything that phones home. Entertainment only. For the group chat.
